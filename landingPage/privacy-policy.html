Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important.

This application is designed with a local-first approach.

Local Storage

All of your data — including images, settings, projects, and application preferences—is stored locally on your device.

The application does not upload your files or personal data to our servers.

No Account Required

You can use the application without creating an account or providing personal information.

Internet Access

The core functionality works entirely offline.

If the application ever offers optional online features (such as checking for updates or connecting to third-party services), those features will be clearly identified and will never upload your files without your explicit permission.

Your Images

Your reference images remain your property at all times.

Image analysis is performed locally on your device whenever possible. Your images are never shared or transmitted without your knowledge and consent.

Data Collection

The application does not collect or sell your personal data.

If anonymous diagnostic or crash reports are ever introduced, they will be optional and disabled unless you choose to enable them.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes in future releases, the updated version will be published with the application.

 

In short: your images stay on your device, your data stays under your control, and the application is designed to respect your privacy by default.
