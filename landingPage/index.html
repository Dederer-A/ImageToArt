Tools & Filters

Each layer acts as a flexible, non-destructive filter designed to analyze and enhance your artwork.

🎨  Tonal Analysis & Light

  • Black & White Instant grayscale conversion to check overall tonal values.
  • Contrast & Levels Fine-tune contrast and tonal levels to spot details in shadows and highlights.
  • Threshold & Posterize Simplify values and group shapes into defined tones.
  • Squint Blurs the image to evaluate composition and mass without getting distracted by details.
  • Gamma & Saturation Precise adjustments for image gamma and color vibrancy.

📐  Composition & Layout

  • Rule of Thirds Classic compositional grid for balanced element placement.
  • Golden Ratio Advanced dynamic grid based on the golden spiral for harmonious framing.
  • Grid Customizable grid overlay for accurate reference transfer and proportion checking.

🔍  Details & Styles

  • Edge Extracts outlines and boundaries for precise line-art analysis.
  • Blur Smooth blurring for soft-form and value assessment.
  • False Color Maps luminance to false colors for clear lighting range breakdown.
